## Case Overview

The Accra Beach Hotel is a 175-room beachfront hotel on the south coast of Barbados, close to the airport and the capital, Bridgetown. Its rooms break down as:

| Room type | Count |
|---|---|
| Standard rooms (Island, Pool and Ocean View) | 141 |
| Ocean View Junior Suites | 24 |
| Two-bedroom Suites | 4 |
| Penthouse Suites | 6 |

The hotel has traditionally been a resort destination, but it now also draws a large **corporate segment**, alongside **tour operators** and **individual travelers**. Demand is seasonal: occupancy and rates peak from January to March and are lowest in the summer months.

## The Decision

Cherita Howard, the hotel's sales manager, has been asked by the **West Indies Cricket Board (WICB)** to make the Accra Beach the host hotel for the West Indies Cricket Home Series (India and New Zealand visiting). The WICB wants a discounted block of rooms booked more than six months ahead, in exchange for marketing exposure in its promotional materials and televised matches.

**Core question:** Should the hotel accept the WICB's offer, and on what terms?

## Key Facts and Figures

**The WICB request**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Rooms requested | 50 standard rooms for the duration of each part |
| Rate offered | $110 per room per night |
| Rooms per team | Each team must be housed on a single floor |
| Laundry | Free laundry for team uniforms and practice gear |
| Laundry cost to hotel | About $20/day in-house, about $200/day if outsourced |

**Series schedule**

| Part | Opponents | Dates | Nights |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | West Indies vs. India | Apr 24 to May 7 | 14 |
| II | West Indies vs. India | May 27 to May 30 | 4 |
| III | West Indies vs. New Zealand | Jun 17 to Jun 26 | 10 |

- Total nights across the three parts: **28**
- Total room-nights requested: **28 × 50 = 1,400**
- Gross room revenue at $110: **1,400 × $110 = $154,000**
- Laundry cost over 28 days: about **$560** in-house or **$5,600** outsourced
- Normal rate for these dates, per management: **$140 to $150** (Exhibit 7 shows last year's ADR on these dates ranging from about $101 to $138)

**Hotel concerns noted in the case**

- Displacement of higher-paying guests on busy dates
- Lower food and beverage spend from team members on per diem budgets
- Possible reaction of other guests to the cricket teams
- Substantial marketing and brand exposure as the official host hotel
